Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide when you contact us or submit a website form. Depending on the request, this may include your name, company or organization, email address, phone number, facility or equipment details, and the contents of your message.

Our website hosting and security providers may also process limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, request timestamps, and server logs as needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

How we use information

We use information for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • respond to inquiries and requests for certification or service;
  • prepare estimates, coordinate scheduling, and communicate about work;
  • maintain business, customer-service, and compliance records;
  • protect the website and our systems from misuse, fraud, or security threats; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable agreements.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral advertising trackers on this website. Infrastructure providers may use essential technical mechanisms or server-side logging required to deliver and secure the site. If we add analytics or non-essential tracking in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

When information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that support normal business operations, including website hosting and transactional email delivery. Our current website infrastructure may include Vercel for hosting and Resend for delivery of contact-form email.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful process, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets.

Data retention

We retain inquiry and business information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, satisfy legal or insurance requirements, resolve disputes, and protect our legitimate interests. Retention periods may vary based on the nature of the information and the relationship.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. However, no website, email service, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

Your privacy choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted to us and to request correction or deletion where appropriate. Some information may need to be retained for legal, contractual, insurance, security, or ordinary business-record purposes.

We do not knowingly use this website to collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-party services

Third-party providers maintain their own privacy practices and terms. Use of their services is subject to those providers’ policies. This policy applies to American Air Flow’s handling of information through this website and does not control independent third-party websites or services.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
