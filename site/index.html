Standards

NSF/ANSI 49

Defines protective performance for Class II biological safety cabinets, including downflow, inflow, and HEPA integrity criteria.

ASHRAE-110

Establishes tracer gas methodologies to quantify containment effectiveness of laboratory fume hoods in real-world conditions.

ANSI Z9.5

Guidance for laboratory ventilation design, commissioning, and recurring verification across research facilities.
